Pet care testing release · 20 October 2026

Your pet records and privacy

My pets saves the details and photos you enter in this browser on this device. An account is not required. Automatic cloud backup and synchronisation are not active.

What is saved

Your pet profiles, resized photos, microchip and council registration numbers, usual vet, contact details, care notes, owner-entered visit history and reminders are stored in this site's local browser database. They are separate from Mucka product captures and the fictional Plus demo.

What leaves your device

This release does not upload pet records or selected photos to Mucka, a veterinary practice or an AI service. The site host still receives normal website requests, such as page and image requests. My pets loads its font from Google Fonts. On Netlify testing previews, Netlify may also add its own toolbar; that toolbar is separate from pet-record storage.

If you download a backup, your records and pet photos are included in that file. If you export reminders, the selected pets' names, reminder titles, notes and due dates are included in the calendar file. Importing that file into a calendar places those details in the calendar service you choose.

Keep a backup

Use Backup & data to download a copy and keep it somewhere private. A backup can be restored on another device through the same menu. Restoring replaces the pet records already saved there, and asks you to confirm first. Back up both devices before replacing anything.

Clearing website data, private browsing, device loss or browser storage removal can remove local records. A Safari record does not automatically appear in Chrome or on your computer. The app does not currently encrypt its local database or downloaded backup separately from your device and browser protections.

Editing and deleting

You can edit or delete pets, visit notes and reminders. Deleting a pet also removes its stored photo, visits and reminders from this browser. Deletion asks you to confirm. It does not remove backup files you have downloaded or calendar events you have imported; remove those copies separately when needed.

Reminder limits

Mucka shows pending reminders and creates the next date when you complete a repeating reminder. Calendar export includes each next pending event with a one-hour alert. You must import the file and check the calendar's alert settings. Mucka does not deliver push notifications in this release, and exports are not an automatically synchronised calendar feed.

Photo Studio

Studio displays prepared example portraits. Selecting a photo there keeps it in the tab and does not transform or upload it. A photo saved to a pet profile is resized to a JPEG on your device and included in the pet backup. Live AI portraits and payments are not connected.

Using the records

Microchip and registration fields store what you type; they do not register a pet, validate ownership or update official records. Visit notes belong to the owner and are separate from a veterinary clinical record. Reminder dates should be checked against your vet's advice or the relevant renewal notice.
